# Building a course: student guide

Your instructor will give you the web address and your login. Work in a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

## 1. Start a course
1. Log in and choose **Add new course**. Give it a clear title.
2. A course is made of **pages** (the lessons on the home screen). Each page has **sections**, and a section holds **blocks**, and each block holds **components** (text, image, video, quiz question…).
3. Add a page, then open it and add content. Use **Preview** often.

## 2. Content you can use
- **Text, images, video:** add a component and pick Text, Graphic or Media. Every image needs **alt text** (a short description for people who cannot see it). Decorative images can be marked as such.
- **YouTube or Vimeo:** use the YouTube or Vimeo component and paste the video address.
- **Interactions:** accordion (expand/collapse), narrative, hotspot images, tabs, matching.
- **Quiz questions:** multiple choice, image choice, text input, slider. Use the assessment extension to add a score and a results page.
- **H5P activities:** make one at h5p.org or in Lumi, export it as an `.h5p` file, upload it in **Assets**, and add an **H5P Player** component. *Your instructor must approve a new H5P file before it will show.* Preview shows "waiting for approval" until then; you cannot download the course until it is approved, so ask in good time.

## 3. Start from PowerPoint or Google Slides
Export a Google Slides deck first with *File → Download → Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx)*. Then either:
- **Put the deck inside a lesson:** add a **Slides** component to a block, open its settings, click **Import PowerPoint** and pick your file. Its slides are replaced by your deck's slides (title, text and the first picture on each).
- **Start a whole course from the deck:** on the dashboard choose **Import source** and pick the file.

Then finish it in the editor: add alt text to pictures, add quiz questions, and use other components where a plain slide is not enough.

## 4. Check before you submit
1. Click **Check accessibility** and fix what it reports (missing alt text, low contrast, empty headings). Aim for none left.
2. **Preview** the whole course on your phone-sized window as well.
3. Read it aloud once; fix typos.

## 5. Submit
Download as the format your instructor asked for:
- **SCORM** to upload to the LMS, or
- **Web** to open on a website.

Submit the zip file exactly as downloaded. Do not unzip and re-zip it.

## If something goes wrong
Note what you clicked and what you saw (a screenshot helps) and tell your instructor.
